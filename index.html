About Me

I am a B.Tech CSE (IoT, Cyber Security & Blockchain Technology) student who loves to explore the world of technology. I enjoy building web applications, network security tools and AI based solutions. My goal is to become a Cybersecurity Engineer and contribute to building a safer digital world.

My journey started in 2023 when I began my B.Tech, and it didn't take long before I found myself pulled toward two things at once — breaking systems apart to see how they work, and building applications that solve real problems. That curiosity turned into hands-on practice with tools like Wireshark and Nmap for network analysis, and Python, Django and Flask for building full-stack applications. Along the way I've worked on everything from a real-time packet sniffer to an AI-powered voice assistant, picking up certifications in Cyber Security Essentials, Linux, and Ethical Hacking to back up what I was learning hands-on. What I enjoy most is the overlap between the two worlds — writing code that not only works, but is built to withstand the ways it could be broken. That's the mindset I'm carrying forward as I work toward becoming a full-time Cybersecurity Engineer.
